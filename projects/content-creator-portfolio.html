Project Overview

I designed a videography / content creation portfolio within 1-week to meet the client's deadline. The goal was to reflect the client’s unique creative style and effectively sell their work to local employers.

✨ Key Achievements

  • Delivered custom design in 1-week
  • Achieved 100% success rate in user testing (out of 4 users)

Since the client's budget was limited and they didn't need a website that would be indexed in search engines, I recommended a free Wix website to design and host their professional portfolio. Understanding the Challenge.

The client needed a professional portfolio that clearly showcased their relevant work within their community without the cost or complexity of a standard hosted site.

The project required balancing visual creativity with usability while working within Wix’s limitations. It was also important that employers could quickly find key projects, understand the client’s expertise, and view results without confusion.

My approach focused on researching similar portfolios, defining a simple but effective visual style, and planning a structure that prioritized clarity. Early designs were created in Figma, followed by iterative adjustments during development in Wix based on platform constraints and client feedback.

1 / 5

Building a Flexible Portfolio System.

A major challenge was presenting multiple projects clearly within Wix’s limited free portfolio features. This directly impacted how easily users could navigate and understand the client’s work.

Wix’s default portfolio templates lacked customization options needed for clarity, such as structured text formatting. This made it difficult to visually highlight important details like roles, dates, and outcomes. Without a clear system, the portfolio risked feeling cluttered or inconsistent.

To solve this, individual pages were created for each project instead of using the built-in portfolio system. A reusable page template was developed and duplicated to maintain consistency while allowing full control over layout and formatting. Although this required more manual linking between pages, it resulted in a more polished and user-friendly experience.

2 / 5

Improving Navigation and Usability.

Ensuring users could quickly find and view the client’s best work was essential to the portfolio’s success. Poor navigation would reduce engagement and weaken the client’s professional presentation.

Users needed to easily move between projects, locate video links, and access the contact page without confusion. With multiple pages and manual linking, there was also a higher risk of broken navigation or user frustration.

The solution emphasized clear visual hierarchy, simple navigation patterns, and consistent layout design. Thin dividing lines, ample white space, and a minimal color palette helped guide users naturally through the content. Careful attention was given to linking every page correctly, resulting in smooth navigation and a seamless browsing experience confirmed through testing.

3 / 5

Strengthening Visual Identity and Content.

The portfolio needed to reflect the client’s creativity while maintaining a professional and modern feel. A weak visual identity could make the work feel less impactful or memorable to employers.

Color and typography choices played a key role in shaping this perception. The design also needed to ensure readability across devices and highlight important information without overwhelming the user.

A simple palette of black and white with purple and pink accents was used to draw attention to key elements while reinforcing creativity and approachability. The Epilogue typeface provided a clean, modern look with strong readability across screen sizes. Additionally, close collaboration with the client helped refine project selection and write clear summaries that effectively communicated skills and results.

4 / 5

Validating the Final Experience.

Even a well-designed portfolio must prove it works for real users. Without testing, usability issues could go unnoticed and impact the client’s opportunities.

The goal was to confirm that users could complete essential tasks, such as navigating between projects, finding video content, and accessing contact information. Feedback from real users and professionals would help validate the design decisions.

User testing with four participants resulted in a 100% task completion rate, confirming the portfolio’s ease of use. All navigation worked smoothly, and users were able to find information quickly. The project’s success was further reinforced when a hiring manager directly praised the portfolio’s design and user experience.

5 / 5
